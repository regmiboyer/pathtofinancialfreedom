Phase 1 — Build (DCA)
DCA duration
How many years you keep making monthly contributions. DCA = "Dollar-Cost Averaging" — investing a fixed amount regularly regardless of market ups and downs.
yr
Coast duration
Years between stopping contributions and starting withdrawals. You stop adding money but leave it invested — the portfolio "coasts" and keeps growing on its own.
yr
Inflation rate
The expected annual rise in prices (historically ~3%). Used to show you the "real" purchasing power of your portfolio — $1M in 20 years buys less than $1M today.
%
Phase 3 — Withdrawal
Base monthly withdrawal
How much money you plan to take out of your portfolio each month in retirement. This is your starting amount — it increases each year by the withdrawal increase % below.
$
Annual withdrawal increase
How much your monthly withdrawal grows each year, typically to keep pace with inflation. At 3%, a $12,000/month withdrawal becomes ~$12,360 in year 2, and so on.
%
Withdrawal duration
How many years you plan to draw from your portfolio in retirement. The simulation checks whether your money lasts this long — if not, it shows when funds run out.
yr
